**Flood Alert System for Semi-basement Housing** is a concept product addressing the critical semi-basement flooding hazards that caused multiple fatalities in Seoul (Jul – Sep 2024).

**Role:** Concept Developer & Design Lead

**Awards:** 13th SNU Engineering Creative Design Festival Finalist

**Solution:** A three-component integrated safety system:
1. Water-level sensors providing early flood detection
2. Automatic flood barrier deployment
3. Emergency evacuation ramps for rapid egress

**My contribution:**
- Conceptualized the novel three-component system architecture
- Designed and produced user scenario demonstration videos and posters using Adobe Premiere Pro and Adobe Illustrator

**Tools:** Adobe Premiere Pro, Adobe Illustrator, physical prototyping
