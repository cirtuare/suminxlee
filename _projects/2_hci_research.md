**Daily Rhythm Recovery for Socially Isolated Youth** is an HCI research project conducted as part of the *HCI Theory and Practice* course at Seoul National University (Sep – Dec 2025).

**Role:** Researcher (team project)

**Research process:**
- Conducted semi-structured user interviews and affinity diagramming to identify emotional support needs for socially isolated youth
- Designed UI/UX and developed a Figma prototype for an AI chatbot combining daily missions with text/voice interaction
- Participated in comparative user testing and qualitative analysis of intervention approaches

**Methods:** Semi-structured interviews, affinity diagramming, Figma prototyping, user testing
