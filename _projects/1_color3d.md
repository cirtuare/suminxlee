**Color 3D** is a full-stack tactile display system developed to enable visually impaired users to independently perceive colors through haptic feedback.

**Role:** Software Developer & Team Lead (Jul – Nov 2025)

**Awards & Recognition:**
- Speaker at International Conference on Appropriate Technology (ICAT) 2025
- Excellence Award at 2025 SNU Social Responsibility Contest

**What I built:**
- An iOS application featuring multimodal interaction via color-height mapping algorithms, voice-guided interfaces, and on-device AI descriptions
- ESP32 firmware for a 24-motor control system with Bluetooth communication protocol for real-time tactile rendering

**Tech stack:** Swift / SwiftUI, CoreBluetooth, ESP32 (C++), on-device Vision framework
