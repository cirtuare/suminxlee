I am a senior undergraduate at [Seoul National University](https://www.snu.ac.kr/), pursuing a double major in Civil & Environmental Engineering and Information Science and Culture (Intelligent Communications / ECE). I am passionate about building technology that centers the human experience — through HCI research and iOS development.

My research explores **assistive technology and human-computer interaction**: most recently, I led the [Color 3D](#) project, a 3D tactile color display enabling visually impaired users to independently perceive color through haptic feedback, which was presented at ICAT 2025 and won an Excellence Award at the 2025 SNU Social Responsibility Contest.

On the engineering side, I develop iOS applications used by thousands — including [Siksha](https://github.com/wafflestudio/siksha-ios), SNU's dining app, and [Acon](#), a restaurant finder. I also served as Executive Committee and Project Team Leader at [Wafflestudio](https://wafflestudio.com/), SNU's premier web/app development club.
